One-Day Itinerary

  1. 8:30 AM: Breakfast near the Duomo

  4. 2:00 PM: Uffizi or Accademia (timed entry recommended)
  5. 5:00 PM: Walk along the Arno + Ponte Vecchio
  6. Sunset: Piazzale Michelangelo
