Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan a Florence trip as a young traveler—top attractions, budget tips, and smart packing advice.

Must-See Attractions

Top Picks

  • Duomo & Cathedral Square
  • Ponte Vecchio
  • Uffizi Gallery
  • Piazzale Michelangelo
  • Accademia Gallery (Michelangelo’s David)
  • Piazza della Signoria
  • San Lorenzo Market
  • Boboli Gardens

Florence is compact and easy to explore on foot, which makes it ideal for young travelers. Many of the city’s most famous landmarks are clustered together, allowing you to see a lot without relying on transportation.

Start early to avoid crowds at major attractions, then spend afternoons wandering through markets, piazzas, and side streets where local life is most visible.

Budget Tips

Florence can be enjoyed on a budget with smart planning. Many of the best experiences are free or low-cost, especially if you balance museums with casual exploring.

  • Eat panini, pizza slices, and pastries instead of dining out every meal.
  • Take advantage of free viewpoints like Piazzale Michelangelo.
  • Book museum tickets online to avoid higher same-day prices.
  • Refill water bottles at public fountains.
  • Walk whenever possible to save on transportation.

Markets and cafés offer some of the most authentic experiences without the high price tag.

Packing Advice

  • Comfortable walking shoes for cobblestone streets.
  • Light layers for changing temperatures.
  • Portable charger and reusable water bottle.
  • Small crossbody bag or backpack with a zipper.

Florence is a walking city, so comfort matters more than fashion. Choose clothing that works for long sightseeing days and casual meals.

Pro Tip

Bring a lightweight scarf—it’s useful for cooler evenings and required when visiting churches or religious sites.
